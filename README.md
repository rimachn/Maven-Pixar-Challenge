# Maven-Pixar-Challenge

**Project Type:** Data Analysis & Visualization (Power BI)  

**Description:**  
Challenge Objective
For the Maven Pixar Challenge, you'll put your data storytelling skills to work by creating an infographic or interactive visual that showcases the legacy of Pixar films over the last 30 years.

About Data Set
Structured information about every Pixar film from Toy Story (1995) to Inside Out 2 (2024). It lists each film’s creators (story writers, screenwriters, directors, composers, and producers), budget, box-office earnings, critic ratings, Oscar nominations and more.

Data Transformation
Imported row data from 5 different csv files into Power BI. Created required dimension, Calendar and fact table in power query. All columns were reviewed and ensured that the correct data type was applied. Loaded data into power BI (Import mode) and created relationships between tables.

Insight
Pixar is doing business as Pixar Animation Studio, is an American animation studio. So far Pixar has released 28 films with a budget of $4.35bn and Box office is $17.04bn which means the profit is 391.69%.

Is it that all Pixar films are profitable? If not, then which films are in loss and which film has the highest return on investment.

Pixar released their first fully computer-animated feature film “Toy Story” on Wednesday, 22 November 1995 during Thanksgiving which was a real blockbuster. The budget of the film was $30m and earned $394m at the box office which is approximately 13 times of budget. Interestingly, this is the only film that has earned such a high profit 1314.79%, however there are films such as “Finding Nemo” and “Inside out 2” who has made the profit 926.16% and 849.02% which were close but did not cross profit made by Toy Story.

Even though most of Pixar's films have great return on investment, there are few films that have seen losses. The Films “Soul”, “Onward”, “Luca”, and” Turning Red” were released in 2020, 2021 and 2022. During this period covid 19 pandemics have affected the world, and the government has banned on public gathering for the safety of everyone. This in turn has affected the films and underperformed at the box office. The film “Turning Red” has the lowest return on investment that is 12.46% . (Note that we do not have “Luca” budget that is why we don’t know whether film made profit or loss)

Pixar franchises have consistently been profitable. Inside Out 2, released on Friday, June 14, 2024, became the highest-grossing film in both the U.S.-Canada and worldwide markets. Its success demonstrated that sequels can be highly profitable, breaking the previous trend.

Critic reviews tell a different story. IMDb, which is more popular than Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic, has received the highest number of reviews—1,226,415—for Wall-E, with a rating of 8.4 out of 10. However, films that generated the highest profits, such as Toy Story and Inside Out 2, which surpassed gross revenue one million, did not achieve the highest ratings. Similarly, on Rotten Tomatoes, Toy Story 4 holds the highest rating at 97 out of 100, based on 459 reviews. On Metacritic, Inside Out 2 has the most reviews (59) but holds a rating of 73 out of 100. This demonstrates that ratings and profits are not always directly correlated. While some critically acclaimed films receive high ratings, they may not be the most profitable. Conversely, commercially successful films do not always achieve the highest ratings from critics or audiences.

Pixar has a history of releasing its movies in June and mostly on Fridays, especially during the summer blockbuster season. This strategy helps them capitalize on school vacations, maximizing audience turnout.

Andrew Stanton has been a major creative force at Pixar Animation Studios since 1995 and has played different roles in Pixar's success. He has been a part of 20 films out of 28 Pixar's films.

Pixar’s films have been nominated 58 times for Oscar awards and have won 18 awards. Out of 58, 30 times were nominated for “Animated Features” and won 11 Animated Feature film awards. Other 7 awards were received for sound editing, original song, original score and special achievement award.
